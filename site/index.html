Humanoid Logbook

We are teaching a robot arm not just to execute tasks but to learn them: Seeed reBot B601-RS, LeRobot, teleoperation with a leader arm, recording demonstrations and training locally on a Mac mini. This logbook records every working day – mindset, wins, fails and learnings – so you can rebuild it yourself.
