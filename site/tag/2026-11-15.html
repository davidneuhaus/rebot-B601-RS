Day 1 · Sunday, November 15, 2026

From the first teleop script to collision protection

Teleoperation runs on the Mac mini – and we learn why a robot arm needs collision protection.

⏱ 3 min read · Working time: roughly 10–11 h span with breaks (estimate)

A marathon Sunday on the Mac mini: from an empty install to the first recorded demonstration.

🛠What we did

  • 12:15 install (venv, LeRobot 0.7.0, Seeed plugin 1.0.5), 12:58 first teleop script with at most 5° per cycle, 13:04 first GUI with start/stop.
  • Gripper levels ("careful/medium/strong"), a preflight check (leader, CAN, all 7 motors) and automatic leader port detection.
  • Our own collision protection ("guard"): v1 → v2 with compliant rotary joints → v3 → v3.1 → v3.2 with a gravity model. Plus a rollback system with about 18 snapshots.
  • In the evening two live camera views in the GUI, the iPhone as a webcam via a browser bridge, and the first pick-and-place demos.
Video coming soon: teleop clip from the working day

💭Mindset

Our goal: understand learned instead of programmed control – guide the arm with a leader, record demos, train a policy later. Safety comes first: the arm must not keep pushing against obstacles or people.

The obvious rule "tracking error > 10°" does not work, because the target is capped at 5° per cycle anyway. So v1 detects contact via leader distance and missing progress, later via torque.

🏆Wins

  • Gripper "careful" final: kp 10, closing max. 1.1 Nm, holding 1.0 Nm – "works great". Before, it only closed partially (kp 4, 0.8 Nm); at 1.5 Nm we crushed cardboard.
  • The stop hum is gone: a SIGKILL in the middle of the rest move left the motors energized. Now there is a SIGINT, a kill only after 25 s, then all motors are torque-free.
  • Guard v2 passed 40/40 offline tests, v3 74/74.
  • The iPhone Continuity Camera is invisible to OpenCV/LeRobot – the browser bridge is the workaround.

💥Fails

  • Crash on exit: safe_zero sent only subsets of the joints while all 7 were expected → ValueError. Fixed with a plugin patch.
  • Gripper zero: offsets of 13.3° and −38.3° (encoder wraparound after power-off with an open gripper) aborted startup. Now it drives to the closed stop on every start.
  • Guard v1 did not detect the hand: the arm pushed it away (pan max. 9.8° at a 10° threshold). Normal fast motion reached up to 42°/32° deviation – the gap between "normal" and "contact" was too small.
  • False freezes with v3.x. The gravity model in v3.2 should help; it is not yet verified on the real arm. We did not record exactly how often false alarms happened.

📌Learnings

  • A 5° step limit makes tracking error useless as a collision indicator. Better: torque residual against a gravity model.
  • Friction matters: with a low kp the gripper stops short of "closed".
  • Rollback snapshots per change saved us time several times.
  • The emergency stop is currently a switchable power strip – a 48 V mushroom switch is on the list.

🔗Sources

Seeed plugin and docs for the reBot B601-RS, LeRobot, our own session logs (replay), ISO/TS 15066 as a concept reference for later.

</>Code status

Teleop app 3.1.1 → 3.2 and guard v3.2 on the Mac mini; all guard versions up to v3.2 are not yet validated on the real arm.
