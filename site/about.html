About the project

Why this logbook?

This logbook documents David Neuhaus's Humanoid Project. According to our notes the project started on November 9, 2026. There is one entry per working day with more than about an hour of work – and it is honest: with failures, uncertainties and estimated working times.

Every entry follows the same structure so you can follow the project step by step. There are no work logs for November 14 or November 17–20; undocumented work may exist there.
